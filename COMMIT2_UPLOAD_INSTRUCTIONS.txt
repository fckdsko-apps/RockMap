WHAT THIS FIX CHANGES
- Research is organized into Mineral Evidence, Geology, and Area Research.
- Mineral search and Analyze visible mineral evidence are immediate first-level actions.
- Field now has an explicit Prospecting Areas screen.
- A saved Prospecting Area has an immediate Analyze this area action.
- Field Records use Research this location and then ask for radius.
- Analyze around a point explicitly offers map center, Current GPS, Field Record, and entered coordinates, then radius choices.
- Geology results group repeated source polygons by geologic unit instead of repeating the same unit hundreds of times.
- Normal geology rows use compact rock/lithology and the most-specific age; raw SGMC hierarchy/provenance is under Technical & source details.
- The grouped-unit screen does not dump individual source-polygon rows; all polygons remain on the map and in export.
- Geology searches/spatial queries no longer impose the old artificial 500/1000/3000 result caps. All grouped results remain browseable in the page scroll.
- Research GeoJSON/CSV keep the underlying source polygons and provenance, including source scale.
- The old unknown-size live geology install/refresh path is removed from the Android runtime. If geology is absent, Mineral Evidence still works and the app directs users to Data rather than starting an undisclosed download.
- Privacy copy now states that additional RockMap data packs disclose size before download and are size/SHA-256 verified before activation.
