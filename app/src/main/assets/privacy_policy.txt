ROCKMAP PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 2, 2026

RockMap is an offline-first Android field-map and research service. A RockMap account is required to use the Android app. This policy explains how RockMap and its service providers access, use, store, and share information when you create an account, start the RockMap trial, purchase lifetime access, use the Android app, or contact RockMap.


PRIVACY SUMMARY

RockMap stores the minimum account information needed to authenticate you and verify access, including your email address, RockMap account identifier, trial status, and lifetime-purchase entitlement. Payment details are entered with Stripe. RockMap does not require a name or postal address as part of your RockMap profile and does not store your complete payment-card number or CVC.

GPS locations, tracks, saved locations, Field Records, Prospecting Areas, notes, measurements, and attached-photo references remain local to your device unless you explicitly export or share them.


DEVELOPER AND PRIVACY CONTACT

RockMap is developed and distributed as the RockMap project by fckdsko-apps. For privacy questions, account-data requests, deletion requests, support, or other private inquiries, contact contact@rockmapp.com.

The RockMap GitHub issue tracker may be used for public bug reports or technical issues, but users should not post sensitive personal information there.


ROCKMAP ACCOUNTS

A RockMap account is required to sign in to and use the RockMap Android app. RockMap collects and stores the account information needed to authenticate you and determine whether your account is in a trial period or has lifetime access.

RockMap account records may include:

- your email address;
- a RockMap user or account identifier;
- authentication and session information needed to keep you signed in and protect the account;
- account creation and relevant authentication timestamps;
- the beginning and end of your RockMap trial;
- lifetime-access, purchase, refund, and entitlement status; and
- Stripe customer, checkout, payment, or transaction identifiers needed to associate a successful purchase or refund with your RockMap account.

RockMap does not require a name or postal address as part of your RockMap account profile. RockMap does not operate a cloud-sync service for your field research data.


7-DAY TRIAL AND $55 LIFETIME PURCHASE

RockMap is sold as a one-time lifetime purchase, not a recurring subscription. To start the 7-day free trial, you provide a payment method through Stripe on the RockMap website. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, Stripe automatically charges $55 once for lifetime RockMap access. After that purchase, RockMap's refund policy allows 7 days to request a 100% refund of the original RockMap purchase.

RockMap uses trial, payment-status, refund-status, and entitlement information to determine whether your account is allowed to use the Android app. A refund or reversed payment may cause the corresponding lifetime entitlement to be removed.


PAYMENT PROCESSING BY STRIPE

RockMap uses Stripe to process the trial payment method and the one-time lifetime purchase. Payment-card numbers, CVCs, billing details, names, postal addresses, and other payment information requested during checkout are collected and processed by Stripe under Stripe's own terms and privacy practices.

RockMap does not store your complete payment-card number or CVC. RockMap also does not use your Stripe billing name or postal address as RockMap profile information. RockMap receives and retains only the information reasonably needed to associate the transaction with your RockMap account and maintain the correct entitlement, such as transaction identifiers, Stripe customer identifiers, payment status, purchase date, refund status, and related records.


LOCATION DATA

RockMap can access approximate or precise device location when you invoke GPS features.

- Approximate location can be used to show your current position on the map.
- Precise location is used when you choose to center on GPS, save a field waypoint or Field Record, run location-based Research, or use other GPS-dependent Field features.
- If you explicitly start Track Recording, RockMap runs a visible Android location foreground service so the track can continue while RockMap is temporarily in the background. Android displays an ongoing track-recording notification until recording stops.
- RockMap does not request Android ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION permission.
- Your GPS position and recorded track points are stored locally for RockMap's field functions and are not sent to RockMap's account or entitlement servers.

Location access is optional for non-GPS functions. Denying location permission does not cause RockMap to upload location through another method.


LOCAL FIELD AND RESEARCH DATA

Saved locations, Field Records, recorded track points, saved Prospecting Areas, trip plans and stops, sample identifiers, notes, measurements, and related coordinates are stored locally in RockMap's private app storage. This field and research data is separate from your RockMap account and lifetime entitlement.

RockMap does not automatically upload this local field data to RockMap-operated account servers. Signing in to RockMap does not cause your saved field data, GPS history, research areas, tracks, or notes to be synced to a RockMap cloud account.


PHOTOS, IMPORTED FILES, AND EXPORTS

If you attach a photo to a Field Record, RockMap stores the Android document URI you selected and retains read access when the selected provider permits it. The original photo remains managed by the storage/photo provider you selected; RockMap does not automatically upload it to RockMap's account servers.

When you explicitly export data, Android's system document picker lets you choose where to save the file. When you explicitly import a supported GPX, KML, GeoJSON, or RockMap file, RockMap reads the selected file to add supported locations, tracks, or polygon areas to local app storage. RockMap does not automatically upload imported or exported field content to RockMap-operated servers.

If you choose a cloud-storage or sharing provider in Android's document/share UI, that provider receives or serves the selected file under its own terms and privacy practices.


LOCAL SAFETY ACKNOWLEDGMENT TOKEN

Before RockMap can be used, the current release requires acknowledgment of RockMap's Safety & Data Limitations notice unless you previously chose not to show the current disclosure version again.

RockMap stores a randomly generated local acknowledgment token together with the disclosure version, last acknowledgment time, acknowledgment count, and reminder preference. This token is stored only in RockMap's private app preferences. It is separate from your RockMap account identifier, is not an advertising or cross-app tracking identifier, and is not sent to RockMap's account servers. The safety preferences are excluded from Android backup/device transfer. The token is not a server-verified, tamper-proof, or legal-signature record and can be removed by clearing RockMap app data.


ANDROID BACKUP AND DEVICE TRANSFER

RockMap allows Android's standard backup and device-transfer systems to include eligible local databases containing saved markers, trips, and field data. Depending on your device, Google account, and backup settings, this may cause eligible local data to be stored or transferred by the Android platform or backup provider.

Downloadable offline map files, downloaded Colorado geology snapshots, reproducible Research-result files, and the local safety-acknowledgment preferences are excluded from Android backup/device transfer. RockMap does not receive or control copies held by the Android backup provider.


OFFLINE-DATA UPDATES AND NETWORK DATA

RockMap is designed to work offline for many mapping and research functions, but account authentication, entitlement checks, and user-requested data-update checks require network access.

Account and entitlement requests may cause RockMap's hosting, authentication, and network service providers to receive ordinary connection information such as IP address, request time, device/network request metadata, and information needed to authenticate the session and determine trial or lifetime-access status.

For release-managed RockMap data, the app retrieves a small update manifest first. The manifest declares the resource filename, exact byte count, and SHA-256 checksum. RockMap displays the relevant download size before you authorize the additional resource download. Downloaded files are not activated unless their byte counts and SHA-256 values match the manifest.

Queryable Colorado geology is distributed as a versioned RockMap SQLite snapshot built from the U.S. Geological Survey State Geologic Map Compilation (SGMC) Colorado polygons. After the Colorado geology snapshot is installed, RockMap geology search and spatial queries operate on the local database. GPS positions, saved Prospecting Areas, Field Record coordinates, search terms, tracks, notes, photos, and other user-created field data are not sent to USGS as part of those local geology queries.

Update hosts and their infrastructure, including GitHub when GitHub-hosted releases are used, may receive ordinary network information such as IP address, request time, and standard HTTP request metadata under that provider's own privacy practices.

RockMap does not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in the current release.


PUBLIC MAP AND RESEARCH SOURCES

RockMap uses public or publicly distributed geographic and research datasets, including OpenStreetMap/Protomaps-derived basemap data and selected data from USGS, BLM, Colorado Geological Survey, Colorado Department of Transportation, and U.S. Forest Service sources. Queryable Colorado geology uses the USGS State Geologic Map Compilation (SGMC), DOI 10.5066/F7WH2N65, as a Colorado-only local snapshot. The Safety & Data Limitations notice lists the source families in more detail.

These datasets describe places and resources. Viewing or querying installed offline source data does not cause RockMap to send your GPS position to those source agencies.


SERVICE PROVIDERS AND DATA SHARING

RockMap does not sell user data and does not use account or field data for third-party advertising.

RockMap uses service providers where necessary to operate the service. These providers may process limited information for the following purposes:

- Stripe: payment-method collection, payment processing, fraud prevention, refunds, and transaction verification.
- Account, authentication, hosting, and network providers: account creation, sign-in, session management, entitlement verification, security, and service delivery.
- Android backup or device-transfer providers: processing eligible local app data according to your device and account settings.
- Cloud-storage or sharing providers you choose: receiving or serving files that you explicitly import, export, attach, or share.

RockMap does not share your locally stored GPS locations, tracks, Field Records, Prospecting Areas, notes, or measurements with Stripe merely because you create an account or make a purchase.


DATA SECURITY

RockMap stores local field data in Android app-private storage. Network connections used for RockMap account access, entitlement checks, payment-status processing, and release-managed downloads are intended to use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Cleartext traffic is disabled in the Android app. Release-managed downloaded data are checked against declared size and SHA-256 values before activation.

No storage, authentication, payment, or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.


DATA RETENTION

RockMap retains account information while needed to operate your account, verify trial or lifetime access, provide support, process refunds, protect the service, and meet applicable legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution obligations.

Transaction records that must be retained for legitimate legal, tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, refund, or dispute purposes may be kept after an account-deletion request for only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Saved markers, Field Records, tracks, polygon areas, trips, notes, and other local field data remain on the device until you delete them in RockMap, clear RockMap app data, or uninstall the app. Exported files remain wherever you chose to save them and must be deleted from that location. Android backups are subject to the backup provider's own retention and deletion practices.


ACCOUNT AND DATA DELETION

You may request deletion of your RockMap account and associated server-side personal information by contacting contact@rockmapp.com. RockMap will delete or de-identify account information that is not required to be retained for legitimate legal, tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, refund, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Deleting your RockMap account may permanently remove your ability to sign in and use the lifetime entitlement associated with that account. Account deletion does not automatically erase field data or exported files stored locally on your device or in locations you selected outside RockMap; those remain under your control and can be deleted separately.


CHILDREN

RockMap is a general-purpose field-map and research utility and is not designed specifically for children. RockMap does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If RockMap learns that an account contains personal information collected from a child contrary to applicable requirements, RockMap will take appropriate steps to delete it.


CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This policy may be updated when RockMap's account system, payment flow, app features, data handling, service providers, or distribution requirements change. The current policy identifies its latest revision date.
