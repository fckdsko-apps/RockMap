RockMap bundled offline Find index

The compact rockmap_place_index.tsv.gz asset is generated during the APK build from
public geographic-name data exposed by the U.S. Geological Survey National Map Gazetteer
and from Colorado Department of Transportation state-highway data.

Only the compact search derivative is bundled in the APK: names, feature categories,
representative coordinates, aliases, and small ranking/context fields. The source GIS
layers and their geometries are not bundled as a second map dataset.

USGS National Map / GNIS geographic names data are public domain. Colorado highway
records are from Colorado DOT. RockMap's rendered basemap remains the separately installed
Protomaps/OpenStreetMap PMTiles data and retains its existing attribution and licensing.

Offline Find is a locator. A result does not establish a route, trailhead, road condition,
access permission, land ownership, collecting legality, or field safety.
