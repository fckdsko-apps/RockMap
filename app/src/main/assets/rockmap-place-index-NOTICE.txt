RockMap on-device offline basemap search index

RockMap's searchable place index is generated on the Android device from the Colorado
PMTiles basemap already installed in RockMap's private app storage. Find does not bundle
or download a second statewide map/geocoder dataset.

The active basemap is derived from Protomaps/OpenStreetMap data. Basemap attribution
remains: Protomaps © OpenStreetMap contributors. OpenStreetMap data is available under
the Open Database License (ODbL):
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Alpha 6.6 scans source zoom 13 from the installed PMTiles and indexes named populated
places, selected landmarks/peaks, and named water features. The resulting compact index
is stored privately on the device and is automatically rebuilt when the active basemap
SHA-256 changes.

No Colorado place-index binary is generated or bundled by the APK build.
